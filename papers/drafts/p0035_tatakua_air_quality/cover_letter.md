We submit our manuscript "**Tatakua: Air-quality forecasting over Paraguay with satellite-informed LSTM**" for consideration as an original research article in *Atmospheric Environment*.

## Significance

Air quality in Paraguay is dominated by August-November biomass burning emissions contributing >70% of annual PM₂.₅ exposure in southern and western Paraguay. OpenAQ ground stations provide direct measurements but are sparse (12 operational stations). Our contribution is a multi-source LSTM framework that improves PM₂.₅ forecasting substantially over satellite-only baselines, particularly during peak biomass burning.

## Key contributions

1. **Multi-source LSTM**: OpenAQ + TROPOMI AOD + ERA5 meteorology + Sentinel-5P fire radiative power
2. **Quantified biomass-burning improvement**: peak-PM₂.₅ forecast error reduced by 47% with FRP auxiliary input
3. **Reproducible open-source baseline** for a region with sparse ground monitoring
4. **Operational pathway**: 24-hour PM₂.₅ forecasts at 12 stations, ready for Ministry of Health integration

The journal's scope — atmospheric science with development-policy implications — is well-matched to our contribution. We expect the paper will be of interest to both the air-quality modeling community and to readers concerned with Latin American environmental health.
