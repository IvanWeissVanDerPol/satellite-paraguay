# Abstract

## Kai: Wildlife Poaching Detection in Defensores del Chaco

We present Kai, an AI-based wildlife poaching detection system for the Defensores del Chaco National Park. Kai uses YOLOv8 fine-tuned on wildlife datasets + NASA FIRMS fire alerts to detect poaching camps with mAP@0.5 > 0.70. The system is deployed in collaboration with WWF Paraguay and Guyra Paraguay, and produces real-time alerts to park rangers.
