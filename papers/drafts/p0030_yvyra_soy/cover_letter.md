# P0030 Yvyra Soy — Cover Letter

**To:** Editor, *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture*

**Subject:** Manuscript submission — "Yvyra Soy: Indigenous territory
presence and soybean frontier expansion in Paraguay's Gran Chaco
and Eastern regions — A public-data analysis using INE 2022
census, OSM community polygons, and MAG department yield (2007/08--2024/25)"

---

Dear Editor,

We submit for consideration our manuscript, which presents a
reproducible public-data analysis pipeline for joining
administrative census records (INE 2022 indigenous communities,
557 communities across 19 pueblos) with administrative boundary
polygons (OpenStreetMap, 30 features) and agricultural yield
statistics (MAG, 16 soy-producing departments, 2007/08--2024/25).

The contribution is methodology: the pipeline joins three open
Paraguayan data sources that have not previously been combined
in the published literature. The specific measured numbers
(departmental yield, pueblo community counts) are illustrative;
the contribution is the reproducible infrastructure, released
under MIT license at https://github.com/IvanWeissVanDerPol/satellite-paraguay.

We position the paper as methodology infrastructure for
Paraguayan environmental governance, with extensions to (1)
INDI shapefile acquisition for per-community loss analysis,
(2) full-Paraguay Hansen tile integration for spatial overlap,
and (3) FPIC-engaged community co-production per CARE
Principles. The current submission is the public-data baseline.

The paper has been prepared following the journal's data
availability policy. All code, data processing scripts, and the
pipeline are open-source. Raw data sources are cited and
publicly accessible.

We declare no conflicts of interest. The work has not been
submitted elsewhere.
