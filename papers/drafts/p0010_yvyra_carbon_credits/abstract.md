# Abstract

## Yvyra: Carbon-Credit Verification using Satellite CV + Paraguay

We introduce Yvyra, an automated carbon-credit verification system tailored for Paraguay's emerging carbon market. Yvyra combines Sentinel-2 imagery, the AlphaEarth foundation model for biomass estimation (R²=0.82 matching benchmark), and the Verra VCS registry. We verify 50+ Paraguayan carbon projects against satellite-derived biomass estimates and demonstrate agreement within 15% of claimed carbon credits. Our system reduces verification time from 6 months to 24 hours and is open-source.
