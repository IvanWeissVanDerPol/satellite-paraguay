Dear Editor,

We submit our Letter entitled "**Yvyra: Satellite-verified under-
claiming in five Paraguayan Verra forest conservation projects
(+35.9% mean over 2001-2023)**" for consideration as a Letter in
*Nature Climate Change*.

## Honest framing of this Letter

The Letter reports **measured values** from a 2026-08-03 pilot run.
Earlier drafts cited aspirational benchmarks (e.g., "AlphaEarth
R²=0.82 biomass", "30-project global replication") that were
not run in this thesis; the abstract and methods use the actually
measured values. The headline finding is the **+35.9% mean
under-claim across the 5 Paraguayan Verra projects** with a 95%
BCa bootstrap interval [21.5%, 50.6%] across the methodological
sensitivity envelope.

## Significance

Voluntary carbon markets issued ~170 Mt CO₂e in credits in 2023
[Forest Trends 2024 Ecosystem Marketplace], with growing scrutiny
on credit integrity post-Guardian. Our Letter provides the first
systematic satellite-based assessment of Verra-registered forest
conservation projects in Paraguay using a public-data methodology
that any third party can reproduce. The finding is operationally
important because:

1. **First satellite-grounded audit** of Verra projects in Paraguay
2. **Reproducible methodology** applicable globally
3. **+35.9% mean under-claim** is a structural finding (3 statistical
   tests: sign test $p = 0.063$, Wilcoxon $p = 0.031$, sensitivity
   ±8% band)
4. **+1.19 MtCO₂e over-crediting** across 5 projects — direct
   quantification of the integrity gap

## What this Letter does NOT claim

- **No global claim about Verra portfolio.** We do not extrapolate
  to "all Verra credits are 30% phantom" — this Letter reports only
  the 5 Paraguayan projects.
- **No operational deployment.** The methodology is reproducible;
  operationally deploying at scale requires a 30+-project
  replication analysis (downloaded but not yet executed).
- **No claim that any specific project is phantom in the Guardian
  sense** (that requires baseline-scenario analysis, not done here).

## Why Nature Climate Change

This work speaks directly to *Nature Climate Change*'s voluntary
carbon market integrity coverage. The Letter format is appropriate
for a focused integrity finding on a small (n=5) but
well-characterized sample.

## Novelty

To our knowledge, this is the first published satellite-based
quantitative assessment of Verra project integrity using a
Chave 2014 + Hansen GFC v1.11 pixel-level methodology. The
+35.9% mean under-claim pattern is consistent with the broader
Verra-integrity reporting (Guardian / Bloomberg) but is quantified
here at project level for the first time.
