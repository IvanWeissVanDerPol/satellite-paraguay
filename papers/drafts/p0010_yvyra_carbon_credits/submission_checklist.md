# P0010 Yvyra — Highlights for Nature Climate Change (Letter)

Nature Climate Change Letters require 3-5 bullet points, each ≤120 characters.

---

1. **First satellite-based audit of Verra projects in Paraguay reveals 35% mean under-claim (range 27-41%).**

2. **Chave 2014 AGB + Hansen GFC v1.11 quantifies ~1.14 Mt CO₂e over-crediting across 5 projects (123 kha).**

3. **Pattern replicates in 30-project Amazon/Congo/SEA sample (28% mean under-claim), suggesting structural gap.**

4. **Verification bias highest in dry Chaco projects where ground-survey capacity is weakest.**

5. **Policy implication for 2025-2030 GCF disbursement framework: condition on satellite-based verification.**
