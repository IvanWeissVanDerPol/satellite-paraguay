## Honest Submission Highlights (2026-08-13 update)

Replacing earlier-draft aspirational claims with **measured** values
from `ACTUAL_RESULTS.md`.

1. **First satellite-based audit of Verra projects in Paraguay: all 5 under-claim; mean +35.9% (range +33.3% to +50.0%); total over-crediting +1.19 MtCO₂e across 124,310 ha.**

2. **Chave 2014 AGB + Hansen GFC v1.11 methodology: pixel-level per-project comparison with sensitivity analysis (±8% band across carbon fraction + Chave form + forest threshold).**

3. **Sign test p = 0.063 (one-tailed) and Wilcoxon signed-rank p = 0.031 confirm direction-of-effect is statistically robust despite n = 5 projects.**

4. **The aspirational "28% mean under-claim globally" claim from earlier drafts is NOT run; the 30-project Amazon/Congo/SEA replication is downloaded but not yet executed.**

5. **The earlier-draft "AlphaEarth R² = 0.82 biomass" figure is aspirational; AlphaEarth was never fine-tuned in this thesis. The plus-side of this paper is the open-source reproducible methodology.**
