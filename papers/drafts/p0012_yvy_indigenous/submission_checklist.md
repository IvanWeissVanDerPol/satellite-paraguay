# P0012 Yvy — Highlights for World Development

World Development requires 3-5 bullet points, each maximum 110 characters.

---

1. **First quantitative satellite-grounded assessment of indigenous territory deforestation in the Paraguayan Chaco.**

2. **Ten territories average 24.7% loss — 3.0× the national rate of 8.5% (95% CI [1.72, 4.20]×, p<0.001).**

3. **Two territories lost ~half their forest (Carmelo Peralta 49.45%, Bahía Negra 49.43%).**

4. **Reverse tenure-effect contradicts global pattern (Garnett 2018) — Statute 904/81 alone insufficient.**

5. **Policy implications: 2025-2030 Forestry Plan + GCF disbursements conditional on FPIC compliance.**
