Key finding: Occidental Chaco has 2.0x the mean yield trajectory of Oriental
despite having only 24% of the indigenous communities. Departments with higher
indigenous community density show faster soy expansion.

Cross-department yield trajectory vs historical mean yield (n=14):

  Departments with HIGHER historical yield have SLIGHTLY SLOWER yield expansion
  (Pearson r = -0.306, p = 0.27)
  Suggestive of yield convergence among the 14 soy-producing departments.
