## Abstract

This thesis presents a unified Python package for satellite-based
environmental monitoring of Paraguay, producing 6 peer-reviewed papers
on deforestation, carbon credits, indigenous land tenure, soybean
yield, wildlife poaching, and air quality. The work demonstrates that
foundation models (Prithvi, AlphaEarth, LLaVA-1.6) can be effectively
transferred to Paraguay-specific tasks with limited training data,
achieving F1 scores of 0.83-0.88 and R² of 0.65-0.79. The system is
CARE Principles-compliant for indigenous data and open-source under
MIT license.
