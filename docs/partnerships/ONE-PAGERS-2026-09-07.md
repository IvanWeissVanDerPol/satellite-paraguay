## 4. Verra (Verified Carbon Standard)

**Contacto:** registry@verra.org
**Persona objetivo:** Director of Methodology Development
**Paper relacionado:** P0010 (Yvyra carbon credits)

### Asunto del correo
*Research collaboration request — Satellite validation of REDD+ project carbon claims in Paraguay*

Dear Verra Methodology Team,

I am writing to request data access for my PhD thesis analyzing the integrity of REDD+ carbon credit projects in Paraguay. My preliminary finding (paper P0010) shows a **+35.9% mean under-claim across 5 Paraguayan Verra projects** (95% bootstrap CI [21.5%, 50.6%]).

**What I am requesting:**
1. Project-level data (geographic boundaries, baseline scenarios, monitoring reports) for the 5 Paraguayan Verra-registered REDD+ projects
2. Methodology documents (VM0007, VM0015, etc.) applied to each project
3. Anonymized comparison with projects in other dry-forest biomes (Cerrado, miombo) for context

**What I offer:**
- Full methodological transparency: all my analysis code is open source (CC-BY 4.0)
- Co-authorship on a joint report if Verra is interested
- Independent validation opportunity — I will share my findings with Verra before public release
- Citation of Verra's methodology guidance in my thesis

**Important clarification:** My finding is a *satellite-based estimate* of forest loss, not a direct measurement of carbon stocks. The +35.9% gap may reflect (a) conservative claiming by project developers, (b) Hansen over-estimation, or (c) genuine under-claim. I am not accusing Verra of fraud — I am asking whether this signal warrants a methodology revision.

I would be happy to present my findings via video call before any publication.

Best regards,
Iván Hocht-VonDerPol
PhD candidate, FADA-UNA, Paraguay

### Subject
*Research submission — Implications for Article 6.4 REDD+ methodology*

Dear Supervisory Body,

I am submitting my PhD thesis findings as technical input to the Article 6.4 REDD+ methodology development.

**Key findings (relevant to Article 6.4):**
1. **+35.9% mean under-claim** across 5 Paraguayan REDD+ projects (Verra, but methodology generalizes)
2. The under-claim is structural, not project-specific — it appears across multiple methodologies
3. Conservative claiming explains only ~25% of the gap (based on the Sentinel-1 radar validation analysis)

**Recommendations for Article 6.4 REDD+ methodology:**
1. **Mandatory independent satellite verification** at project scale (not just national)
2. **Stratified buffer pool**: 40% for projects in deforestation hotspots, 25% for projects in low-pressure zones
3. **Public baseline data**: every Article 6.4 project must publish its baseline scenario and monitoring data in machine-readable format
4. **5-year methodology review cycle** to incorporate new satellite data (Hansen GFC v2, ESA Biomass CCI, GEDI L4A)

**Attachments:** Same as CRCF submission (one-pager + P0010 paper + methodology appendix).
