- Whether you want your name in my thesis
- Any explanation you have for the deforestation I'm seeing

What could go wrong:
- Someone could use this information to pressure your community
- The number I'm reporting might be inaccurate (e.g., if my
  satellite measurement is wrong)
- Other communities or organizations might disagree with how
  I present the finding

How I'm protecting you:
- I will not publish any data about your community without your
  written consent
- You can ask me to remove your data at any time, even after
  publication
- I will share all my analysis methods so you can check them
