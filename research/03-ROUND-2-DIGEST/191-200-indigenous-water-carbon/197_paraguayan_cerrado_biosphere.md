## Background

The Cerrado is a tropical savanna ecosystem:
- **Brazilian Cerrado:** largest (~200 Mha)
- **Bolivia:** medium (~14 Mha)
- **Paraguay:** small (~5-8 Mha, mostly in northeastern Chaco + eastern boundary)

### Ecological significance
- **Tree species:** 800+ tree species
- **Endemic plants:** ~10,000 species
- **Critical biome for agriculture:** 60% of Brazilian soy
