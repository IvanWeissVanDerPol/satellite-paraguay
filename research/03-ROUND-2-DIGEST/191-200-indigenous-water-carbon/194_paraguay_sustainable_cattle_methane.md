- Cattle = ~95% of agricultural emissions (CH4+N2O)

### Mitigation options
- **Animal supplementation:** +10-15% productivity
- **Improved pasture:** +20-40% productivity
- **Stocking rate adjustment:** +10-20% lower GHG/head
- **Manure management:** variable
