## Methodology

### Best practices
1. **Reciprocal relationship:** Indigenous communities contribute local knowledge + receive technical training
2. **FPIC:** Free Prior Informed Consent throughout
3. **Local adaptive protocols:** not imposing Western methodology
4. **Data sovereignty:** Indigenous communities retain rights to data
5. **Co-interpretation:** Joint analysis sessions
