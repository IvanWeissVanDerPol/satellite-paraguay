## Background

Forest connectivity supports:
- Animal migration
- Genetic diversity
- Resilience to disturbances
- Carbon stock persistence

## Mitigation strategies

### 1. Bridge sites
- Forest corridors between protected areas
- ~10 in Paraguay currently

### 2. Restoration
- Active reforestation of connecting areas
- Cost: ~$200-1500/ha
