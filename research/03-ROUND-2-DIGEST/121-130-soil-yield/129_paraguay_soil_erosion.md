# 129. Paraguay Soil Erosion — Degradation Synthesis

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Soil erosion context

Paraguay's eastern region experienced significant soil erosion during the soybean frontier expansion 2000-2010. Today the legacy persists:
- Compaction from heavy machinery
- Loss of topsoil (10-30% over 20 years in degraded areas)
- Reduced water-holding capacity

## Erosion estimates (Paraguay per USLE model)

### Eastern region (Oriental)
- **Mean erosion rate:** 5-15 t/ha/year
- **Hotspot:** Caaguazú, San Pedro, Concepción departments
- **Driver:** Continuous soybean, lack of cover crops

### Chaco
- **Mean erosion rate:** 2-5 t/ha/year
- **Hotspot:** Western Alto Paraguay
- **Driver:** Cattle overgrazing, deforestation

### Total annual loss (nationwide)
- **Estimated:** 25-40 million tonnes/year topsoil

## Implications for Yvutu, Vyrá, Yrupe

1. **Soil erosion = land degradation = lower biomass + lower SOC + lower water retention**
2. **Soil erosion non-linearity:** Once compacted, gains slow significantly
3. **Carbon credit accounting:** If erosion reduces SOC, projects should not claim those gains
4. **Yrupe yield:** Eroded soils under-perform

## Conservation practice references

- **No-till soybean:** Reduces erosion by 50-75%
- **Cover crops:** Reduces erosion by 60-90% (rye, oats)
- **Terracing:** Reduces erosion by 70-95%
- **Agroforestry:** Reduces erosion by 40-80%
