## Paraguay soybean specifics

### Cultivation area
- Eastern Paraguay: 3.5 million ha (~17% of all arable land)
- Chaco region: 1.0-1.4 million ha
- Total soybean area: ~4.5 million ha (2024 estimate)
- 90%+ of Paraguayan soybean = Eastern + Chaco

### Baseline productivity
- **Mean:** 2.5-3.5 t/ha (varies by zone)
- **Best:** 4.5 t/ha (Eastern with irrigation)
- **Worst:** 1.0-1.5 t/ha (Chaco dry years)

### Heat stress impact

| Heat Condition | Yield Loss (%) | Phenology Stage |
|---|---|---|
| Tmax > 32°C during flower | 5-15% | R2-R4 |
| Tmax > 35°C during pod fill | 10-25% | R5-R6 |
| Tmax > 38°C during grain fill | 25-40% | R6-R7 |
| Multi-day heat waves (5+ days above 35°C) | 30-50% | any R stage |

## Implications for Paraguay

Under SSP3-7.0 (RCP equivalent of 7.0 W/m²), the % of Paraguay climate-space that exceeds the soybean heat threshold (35°C during R5-R6) increases:
- **Current (2000-2024):** ~10-15% of growing area
- **2050 (SSP3-7.0):** ~25-35% of growing area
- **2100 (SSP3-7.0):** ~35-50% of growing area

## Mechanism

- **Direct heat stress on flowers:** abortion increases
- **Indirect via water:** ET₀ increases, root water uptake inadequate
- **Combined with high VPD:** combined stress
