# 124. ENSO Impacts on Paraguay — Climate Variability

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## ENSO definitions

- **El Niño:** Warm phase, characterized by anomalous warming of eastern-central equatorial Pacific Ocean
- **La Niña:** Cold phase, characterized by anomalous cooling of eastern-central equatorial Pacific Ocean
- **Neutral:** Neither phase

### Standard indices
- **NINO3.4 SST anomaly**
- **Multivariate ENSO Index (MEI)** (Wolter & Timlin 2011)
- **Oceanic Niño Index (ONI)** — 3-month running mean
- **Southern Oscillation Index (SOI)** — Tahiti-Darwin pressure difference

## Impact on Paraguay precipitation

### El Niño
- **Below-normal precip:** Eastern Paraguay (Oriental) affected first
- **Above-normal precip:** Western Chaco often affected in some patterns
- **Fire risk:** Moderate increase
- **Soybean yield:** Slight decrease (heat stress)

### La Niña
- **Above-normal precip:** Eastern Paraguay (Oriental)
- **Below-normal precip:** Western Chaco
- **Fire risk:** Mixed effects (depends on initial conditions)
- **Soybean yield:** Variable

### Neutral
- **Climatology**
