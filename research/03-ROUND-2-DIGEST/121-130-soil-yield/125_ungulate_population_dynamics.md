## Ungulate species of Paraguay

The Gran Chaco hosts several ungulate species:
- **Marsh deer (Blastocerus dichotomus):** Largest Paraguayan deer, classified Vulnerable (IUCN)
- **Pampas deer (Ozotoceros bezoarticus):** Endangered, declining population
- **Black peccary (Dicotyles tajacu):** Stable population
- **White-lipped peccary (Tayassu pecari):** Vulnerable, declining population
- **Collared peccary (Tayassu tajacu):** Stable
- **Capibara (Hydrochoerus hydrochaeris):** Stable, abundant
- **Lowland tapir (Tapirus terrestris):** Vulnerable
- **Six-banded armadillo (Euphractus sexcinctus):** Stable
