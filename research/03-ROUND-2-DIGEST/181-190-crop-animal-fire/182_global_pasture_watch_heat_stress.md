## Methods

### Heat stress indicators
- **Air temperature (Tair):** Standard metric
- **Wet bulb globe temperature (WBGT):** Combined Tair + RH + wind + radiation
- **Temperature-humidity index (THI):** Standard for cattle
- **Wet bulb temperature:** Most critical for thermal comfort

### Cattle heat tolerance
- **Bos taurus:** THI > 72 = heat stress
- **Bos indicus:** THI > 80 = heat stress

## Paraguay cattle heat stress

### Implications for cattle productivity
- **Loss per stress event:** 0.5-2% in milk yield, 0.2-1% in growth rate
- **Annual loss estimate in Chaco:** ~5-15% of potential productivity
