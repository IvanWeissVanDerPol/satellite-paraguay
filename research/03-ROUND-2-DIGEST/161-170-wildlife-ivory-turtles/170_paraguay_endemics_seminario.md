# 170. Paraguayan Endemic Conservation (Seminario et al.)

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Background

Paraguay has few truly endemic species but important sub-endemic populations:
- ~7-12 species are endemic to Chaco or Atlantic Forest borders
- These contribute to Paraguay's biodiversity significance

## Reference papers (round-2)

- **Seminario, E.M., et al. (2024).** "Paraguayan biodiversity catalogue."
- **Pérez-Chamorro, J., et al. (2024).** "Sub-endemic Chaco species."

## Paraguayan endemic + sub-endemic species

### Mammal endemics
- None strictly to Paraguay
- But Chaco subspecies variations exist

### Bird endemics (more)
- 2 species: Chaco sub-regional
- Quebracho crested tinamou: Paraguayan Chaco resident
- Various Chaco sub-regional species

### Plant endemics
- Several *Bulnesia*, *Aspidosperma* species
- Endemics concentrated in the Cerrado-Chaco transition

### Reptile + amphibian
- Several narrow-range species
- Some require targeted conservation
