## Paraguay puma population

### Estimate
- **Total population:** ~2,000-3,000 individuals
- **Density:**
  - Forest: ~0.5-1.0 per 100 km²
  - Open Chaco: ~0.2-0.4 per 100 km²
  - Cultivated areas: ~0.05-0.1 per 100 km²

### Threats
1. **Habitat loss:** Primary threat
2. **Retaliatory killings:** Common after cattle depredation
3. **Vehicle collisions:** Road expansion
4. **Illegal hunting:** Some pelts
