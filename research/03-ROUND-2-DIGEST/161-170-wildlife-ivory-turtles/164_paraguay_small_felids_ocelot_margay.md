# 164. Paraguayan Ocelot + Margay + Small Felids (Round-2)

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Background

Paraguay has many small wild cats:
- **Ocelot** (*Leopardus pardalis*)
- **Margay** (*Leopardus wiedii*)
- **Jaguarundí** (*Herpailurus yagouaroundi*)
- **Geoffroy's cat** (*Leopardus geoffroyi*) — Eastern
- **Pampas cat** (*Leopardus colocola*) — uncertain in PY
- **Colocolo cat** (*Leopardus braccatus*) — uncertain

## Status in Paraguay

### Threats
- Habitat loss (1)
- Retaliatory killing (2)
- Rodent control poisoning (3 — can kill cats)
- Vehicle collisions (4)

### Ocelot
- Most abundant small felid
- Densities: ~3-10 per 100 km² in Chaco
- Stable

### Margay
- Less common (arboreal)
- Vulnerable
- Restricted to forest interior
