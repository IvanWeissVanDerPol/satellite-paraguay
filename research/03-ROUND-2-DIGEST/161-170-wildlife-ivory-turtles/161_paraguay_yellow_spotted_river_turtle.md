# 161. Paraguay Yellow-Spotted River Turtle Conservation (Round-2 update)

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Background

**Podocnemis unifilis** (Yellow-spotted river turtle):
- **IUCN status:** Vulnerable (decreasing)
- **Range:** Amazon basin + Guianas + parts of Paraguay + Paraná-Paraguay rivers
- **Habitat:** Slow-moving rivers, lagoons, flooded forests
- **Cultural significance:** Eggs harvested for food in some communities

## Paraguay-specific issues

### Egg harvesting
- **Communities:** On the Pilcomayo + Paraguay rivers
- **Volume:** Variable (less regulated)
- **Conservation:** Some communities under FPIC have regulated harvest

### Threats
- **Habitat loss** (river regulation)
- **Illegal capture** (for food)
- **Sand mining** (destruction of nesting beaches)
