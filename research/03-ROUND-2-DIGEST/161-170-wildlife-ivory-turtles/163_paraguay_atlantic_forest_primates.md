# 163. Paraguay Atlantic Forest Primate Conservation (Toepfer et al.)

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Background

Eastern Paraguay Atlantic Forest is part of the southernmost range for several primates:
- **Black howler monkey** (*Alouatta caraya*)
- **Brown howler monkey** (*Alouatta guariba clamitans*)
- **Azara's owl monkey** (*Aotus azarae*)
- **Tufted capuchin** (*Sapajus nigritus*)
- **Black-tufted marmoset** (*Callithrix penicillata*)
