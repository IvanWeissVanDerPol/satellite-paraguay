## Dry Chaco forest composition

### Key species
- *Schinopsis quebracho-colorado* (Quebracho colorado)
- *Aspidosperma quebracho-blanco* (Quebracho blanco)
- *Bulnesia sarmientoi* (Palo Santo)
- *Caesalpinia paraguariensis* (Guayacán)

### Biogeography
- **Western Chaco:** Dominated by cerrado-espinal
- **Eastern Chaco:** Mixed with Atlantic Forest elements

### Conservation implications
- Chaco dry forest is one of the most threatened biomes in South America
- Loss rate: ~2-4% per year in core areas
- Less than 35% of original range remains
