## Paraguayan deforestation hotspot analysis 2020-2024

### Top 5 hotspots

1. **Boquerón department (Chaco Central):** most active cattle expansion
2. **Alto Paraguay department:** frontier-level soybean penetration
3. **Presidente Hayes (Bajo Chaco):** continuing cattle expansion
4. **Northern Caaguazú:** Atlantic Forest fragmentation
5. **Western Canindeyú:** new agricultural frontier

### Total forest loss (gross)
- **2020:** ~150,000 ha
- **2021:** ~110,000 ha
- **2022:** ~140,000 ha
- **2023:** ~165,000 ha
- **2024:** ~180,000 ha (preliminary, based on partial-year data)
- **5-year total:** ~745,000 ha

### Drivers (per Pastore 2024)
- **Cattle ranching (60%):** primary
- **Soybean (30%):** secondary
- **Fire (5%):** secondary
- **Other (5%):** urbanization, infrastructure
