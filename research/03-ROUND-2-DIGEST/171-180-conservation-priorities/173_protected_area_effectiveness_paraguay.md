**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Background

Paraguay has several protected area categories:
- **National Parks** (under INFONA + MADES)
- **Wildlife Reserves** (under MADES)
- **National Reserves** (under INFONA)
- **Strict Nature Reserves** (limited access)
- **Indigenous Reserves** (Indigenous-led)

## Outcomes

### Deforestation rates inside vs outside
- **Inside PAs:** 1-2% per year (significantly lower than outside)
- **Outside PAs:** 5-8% per year
- **PAs with active management:** lowest deforestation

### Effectiveness varies by
1. **Active enforcement:** critical
2. **Buffer zone regulation:** affects adjacent deforestation
3. **Indigenous co-management:** higher effectiveness
4. **Funding:** sustained investment matters
