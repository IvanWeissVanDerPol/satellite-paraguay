# 180. Paraguay Water-Energy-Food Nexus Synthesis (Round-2)

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Background

The **Water-Energy-Food (WEF) Nexus** is a framework for understanding trade-offs:
- Water resource use = constraints on Energy + Food
- Energy production = impacts on Water + Food
- Food production = impacts on Water + Energy

## Reference papers (round-2)

- **Fader, M., et al. (2024).** "Tropical WEF nexus in South America."

## Paraguay WEF nexus context

### Water
- Average annual renewable water: 388 km³ (per capita)
- Agricultural withdrawals: ~80% of total
- Domestic: 12%
- Industrial: 8%

### Energy
- Hydroelectric (Itaipu): large share
- Distributed solar: growing
- Imports: limited

### Food
- Calorie supply: 3,200/day/person (above avg)
- Domestic production exceeds domestic consumption
- Major exports: soybean (60% of total food exports), beef (25%)

## Key trade-offs

### 1. Land use
- Paraguay has 24+ Mha agricultural land potential
- ~14 Mha currently cultivated
- Expansion pressures on Chaco forest

### 2. Irrigation
- River-based irrigation: expanding pressure on Pilcomayo, Paraguay rivers
- Limited groundwater use
- WEF conflict: agriculture consumes water

### 3. Energy
- Hydroelectric: drought-vulnerable
- 2021 drought reduced Itaipu output by 30%
