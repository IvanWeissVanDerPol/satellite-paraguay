## Background

Community forestry in Paraguay has formal recognition:
- **Law 3001/06:** Articles 19-21 establish PES framework
- **INFONA 2024:** Registered community forestry plans

## Community forestry in Paraguay

### Forest Stewardship Council
- 17 certified operations in Paraguay (most in Chaco)
- Average size: 5,000-50,000 ha
- Products: Timber, charcoal, sustainable forestry

### Community-led agricultural-forestry projects
- Several examples of native community-led silvopastoral systems
- Best practice: Bajo Chaco Indigenous-led silvopastoral
