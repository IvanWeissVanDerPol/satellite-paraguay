# 178. Paraguay Translations of National Strategies (Round-2)

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Current Paraguayan sub-strategies important for thesis

### 1. Plan Nacional Forestal (National Forestry Plan)

Goals to 2030:
- Establish sustainable forest management on 80% of remaining forest
- Reduce deforestation to 50% below 2020 baseline
- Increase reforestation to 75,000 ha/year
- Carbon credit targets: 30 MtCO2e issued by 2030

### 2. Plan Nacional de Cambio Climático (Climate Change Plan)

Targets:
- Climate plans at departmental level by 2026
- Adaptation plans at community level by 2030
- Comprehensive monitoring and reporting

### 3. Plan Nacional de Reforestación (Reforestation Plan)

Targets:
- 50,000 ha planted each year
- Plus regeneration on degraded pasture land
- Focus on native species in Chaco

### 4. Plan Nacional de Biodiversidad (Biodiversity Plan)

Targets:
- 30% of Paraguayan territory in protected areas
- 30% recovery of degraded ecosystems
- Comprehensive ecosystem services inventory

### 5. Plan Nacional de Recursos Hídricos (Water Resources Plan)

Targets:
- 80% of rivers monitored
- 60% of wetlands protected
- National water resource accounting
