## National biodiversity strategy

### Components
1. **Information systems** (target: integration by 2027)
2. **Protected areas** (target: increase to 25% by 2030)
3. **Restoration projects** (target: 50,000 ha/year)
4. **Sustainable production** (target: 60% sustainable by 2030)
5. **Climate adaptation** (target: integrated adaptation plans by 2026)

### Targets not met
- 17 of 20 Aichi targets met partially or not at all
- 12 of 23 SDG 15 targets behind
- 0 of 7 Ramsar targets met fully
