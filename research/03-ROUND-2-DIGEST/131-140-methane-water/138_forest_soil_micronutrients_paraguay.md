## Micronutrient pattern

### Native Chaco forest soils
| Element | Mean (mg/kg) | Range | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cu | 12 | 5-25 | Adequate |
| Zn | 28 | 10-45 | Adequate |
| Fe | 12,000 | 5,000-25,000 | Adequate |
| Mn | 350 | 100-700 | Adequate |
| B | 1.2 | 0.4-2.5 | Adequate |
| Mo | 0.8 | 0.2-1.5 | Adequate |

### Converted pasture soils
| Element | Δ from forest | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cu | -20% | Lower in surface |
| Zn | -30% | Often limiting for cattle |
| Fe | -10% | Stable |
| Mn | -5% | Stable |
| B | -25% | Reduced recycling |
| Mo | -15% | Slight decline |

## Implications for cattle nutrition

- Once forest cleared for pasture, Zn supplementation is essential
- Most Paraguayan ranches use mineral supplements (Cu, Zn, Se)
- Without supplementation, cattle productivity declines

## For ecosystem carbon

- Soil micronutrient availability strongly correlates with SOC accumulation
- Boron is especially critical for organic matter turnover
