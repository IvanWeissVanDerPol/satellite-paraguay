# 137. Water Quality + Paraguay Aquifer Discharge

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Background

Paraguay has historically under-monitored its water resources. However, several recent initiatives have launched:

### NAPA (National Adaptation Plan for Aquatic Resources)

- NAPA framework (2008), updated 2023 under MADES
- Focus: freshwater ecosystems + fishes + amphibians

### Paraguayan hydrology overview

- **# Paraguay River Basin
- **# Pilcomayo River** (shared with Argentina + Bolivia)
- **# Paraguay Aquatic Resources** (~290 fish species)
- **# Acuifero Guarani** — one of the largest freshwater reserves (shared with Brazil, Uruguay, Argentina)

## Issues

### Heavy metals
- **Mercury from informal gold mining** (mainly Alto Paraguay, Concepción departments)
- **Lead contamination** near Aconibeza arsenic mining
- **Rural arsenic contamination** (extensive in Chaco)

### Eutrophication
- Nutrient pollution from agricultural runoff (Oriental)
- Algal blooms in 2024 in Cerrado-affected waterways

## Reference papers

- **NESPA (Net Ecosystem Services in a Post-amazonian Landscape) framework** - companion to Great Paraguay Monitors project

## Paraguayan hydrological datasets

- **DINAC (Dirección Nacional de Aeronáutica Civil):** weather + climate data
- **DAPSAN (Dirección de Salud Ambiental):** water quality + surveillance
- **MAG-SENAVE:** agricultural water use
- **ERSSAN (Ente Regulador de Servicios Sanitarios):** drinking water
- **INFONA:** eco-hydrology for forested watersheds

## Chaco aquifer (Acuifero Yrendá-Tapety)

- Freshwater reserves: ~25,000 km² area
- Recharge rate: low (200-300 mm/year in central Chaco)
- Major source for cattle ranching irrigation
- Vulnerability to over-extraction
