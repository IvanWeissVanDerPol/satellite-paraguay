## Background

## Paraguay cocoa agroforestry systems

### Varieties
- **Trinitario (60%)** — Criollo × Forastero hybrids
- **Forastero (35%)** — bulk production (main stream)
- **Criollo (5%)** — premium fine-flavor cocoa

### Traditional systems in Eastern Paraguay
- **Full sun monoculture:** Higher yield but reduced biodiversity
- **Associated shade (timber + cocoa):** Laurácea, Yvy rapeí, Lapachos
- **Agro-silvo-pastoral systems:** Cocoa + cattle + native trees
- **Cabruca system (Bahian model):** Atlantic Forest remnants + cocoa

## Carbon sequestration

| System | SOC (tC/ha in 0-30cm) | Carbon credits |
|---|---|---|
| Forest full | 50-70 | Standard credit |
| Forest + cocoa shade (low intensity) | 40-50 | Eligible for VM0015 |
| Cocoa monoculture | 25-30 | Lower credit |
| Cattle pasture | 15-20 | Negative credit |

## Implications for thesis
