## Paraguay native tree species for reforestation

### Eastern region (humid subtropical)
- Cedrela odorata
- Tabebuia spp
- Cordia americana
- Peltophorum dongo

### Chaco region (dry sub-tropical)
- Aspidosperma quebracho-blanco
- Schinopsis quebracho-colorado
- Bulnesia sarmientoi (Palo Santo)
- Caesalpinia paraguariensis (Guayacán)

## Reforestation success rates

### Chaco (Dry season re-establishment)
- Survival rate: 35-65% (depending on species)
- Growth: 0.3-0.8 m/year (slow)
- Carbon sequestration: 0.3-1.5 tC/ha/year (lagged)

### Eastern (with irrigation)
- Survival rate: 65-85%
- Growth: 1.0-2.0 m/year (fast)
- Carbon sequestration: 1.5-3.5 tC/ha/year

## Implications for Vyrá

- Chaco projects under VM0015 or VM0033 include reforestation
- Native species projects take 5-10 years to register meaningful C sequestration
- Conservation (avoided deforestation) projects more effective at preventing new emissions
