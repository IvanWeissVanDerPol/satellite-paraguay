## What is Woodwell Climate Research Program

- Founded 1985 as Woods Hole Research Center
- Renamed in 2018 to Woodwell Climate Research Program
- Focus: climate mitigation, forests, soil
- Researcher base includes Plevin + Gibbs + Walker
- Key methodology development partner for ICVCM

## Reference publications

- **Walker, W., et al. (2024).** "Improved carbon stock estimation in tropical forests." *Remote Sensing of Environment*.
- **Mitchard, E.T.A. (2018).** "The tropical forest carbon cycle and climate change." *Nature*.
- **Harris, N.L., et al. (2021).** "Global maps of twenty-first century forest carbon fluxes." *Nature Communications*.

## Zarr + Cloud-native geospatial formats

Woodwell Group pioneered the use of cloud-native Zarr for forestry datasets:
- 5 TB forest carbon data previously locked in geotiff
- Zarr allows cloud-based slicing
- Critical for Paraguay-scale analysis at full resolution
