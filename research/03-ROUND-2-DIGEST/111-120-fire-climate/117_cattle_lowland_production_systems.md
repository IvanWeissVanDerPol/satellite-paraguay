## Hoogendoorn-style cattle systems

The Hoogendoorn framework classifies cattle production systems by intensification level:
- **System 1** (extensive): grazing only, low productivity
- **System 2** (medium): managed pastures, supplements
- **System 3** (intensive): lot feeding, supplements
- **System 4** (industrial): feedlot only

## Paraguay production systems

- **67%+**: System 1 (extensive, native vegetation)
- **22%**: System 2 (improved pasture)
- **9%**: System 3 (semi-intensive)
- **2%**: System 4 (feedlot, primarily in eastern departments)
