# 118. Soil Carbon in Chaco — Gran Chaco SOC Dynamics

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Why SOC matters

Soil organic carbon (SOC) is significant for:
1. **Climate change mitigation:** ~25% of GHG mitigation potential via soil carbon sequestration
2. **Agricultural productivity:** SOC improves water retention, drought resilience
3. **Ecosystem services:** SOC underpins biodiversity + soil biodiversity
4. **Carbon credits:** SOC sequestration is a growing carbon credit market (VM0042 et al.)

## Gran Chaco specific SOC dynamics

- **Baseline:** 32-50 tC/ha in upper 30 cm across natural Chaco soils
- **Deforested/converted soils:** ~15-20 tC/ha (40-60% loss)
- **Pasture recovery:** partially accumulates SOC but slower than forest
