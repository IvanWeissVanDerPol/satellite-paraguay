# 116. Argentine + Bolivian Chaco + Brazil Pantanal — Cross-Border Comparison

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Why a cross-border comparison matters

The Gran Chaco spans four countries:
- **Argentina:** ~62% of the Gran Chaco area
- **Paraguay:** ~23%
- **Bolivia:** ~14%
- **Brazil:** ~1% (mostly Pantanal, separate)

Each country has different land tenure policy → different deforestation rates.

## Tenure by country

### Argentina
- Provincial land tenure law 26.331/2007 (Native Forest Law)
- Forest utilization permits required
- Cattle ranching is dominant agricultural use

### Bolivia
- INRA Law (1996) + CPAC (Comité Forestal de Bolivia)
- Land titling ongoing
- Chiquitano dry forest most affected

### Paraguay
- Zero Deforestation Law 2524/04 (Eastern region only)
- Less enforcement in Chaco
- Indigenous territories in process of titling

### Brazil (Pantanal)
- Partly overlapping with Paraguay
- Protected-areas driven (Pantanal Matogrossense National Park)
