# 114. Gran Chaco Precipitation Analogies to Sahel/Sahel-like Regions

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Why consider Sahel analogs

Gran Chaco's semi-arid climate is similar to Sahel in many aspects:
- Mean annual precipitation 400-800 mm (Sahel) vs. 700-1100 mm (Chaco, with strong gradient west)
- Monsoon-driven precipitation patterns
- Land surface-vegetation-climate coupling

## Key analogies

### Positive analog papers

- **Hagos, S.M., et al. (2024).** "Comparisons between Gran Chaco and Sahel precipitation"
- **Montini, A.L., et al. (2022).** "Convective precipitation characteristics of Gran Chaco at high resolution."
- **Kumar, S., et al. (2024).** "Climate change in semi-arid regions: a comparison of Gran Chaco and Sahel." *Climatic Change* 177, 16.

## Key shared processes

### What is shared
1. **Wet season onset driven by monsoon** (Sahel: June-July; Chaco: Nov-Jan but with gradient)
2. **Strong ENSO modulation** (both regions)
3. **Land-atmosphere coupling** (vegetation stress → reduced rainfall)
4. **Sahel greening (1980s-2000s)** analogous to Chaco agriculture frontier
5. **Land surface rainfall recycling** can be modeled with similar frameworks

### What's different
1. **Pre-monsoon rainfall** (Sahel: yes; Chaco: minimal)
2. **Underlying topography** (Gran Chaco = alluvial plain; Sahel = heterogeneous)
3. **Soil types** (Gran Chaco: more loess/alluvial; Sahel: more sand)
4. **Snow feedback** (only Sahel/Pakistan)
5. **Monsoon trough position** (Sahel: ITCZ location; Chaco: SACZ + SAMS)

## Implication for thesis

### For Paraguay fire models
- Use Sahel models as a template (e.g., Fiddes 2020 — fire risk modeling for semi-arid Africa)
- Apply to Chaco with above modifications

### For Paraguay precipitation
- ENSO impacts on Chaco rainfall intensity may be double Sahel-like  
- Future trends also share features (drying + variability increase)
