The Atlantic Forest of eastern Paraguay is **biologically distinct** from Chaco. Important paper context:

- **Rezende, C., et al. (2018).** "Atlantic Forest restoration strategies and the role of climate change."

### Atlantic Forest in Paraguay

The Atlantic Forest of Paraguay extends into eastern departments (Alto Paraná, Itapúa, Caazapá, Guairá, Caaguazú, San Pedro).

## Method implications

Satellite remote sensing of Atlantic Forest differs from Chaco in:
- Haze/cloud cover frequency (Atlantic Forest = cloudy)
- Spectral signatures (more green, less brown)
- Phenology cycles (mid-east Brazilian vs dry Chaco)
