## Climate models covering Paraguay (consolidated)

- **MADES Paraguay climate projections (2026 NDC 3.0):** PDF cached at `/opt/data/profiles/ivan/research/iterations/41-60-yvy/56_paraguay_ndc3_full_text_oct2025.md` (137 pp.). ### 2. CMIP6 + RCMs (regional climate models)

- **ETA-CPTEC 40 km** regional model (Brazilian CPTEC) has Paraguay runs
- **RegCM4 CORDEX-SAM** (South America domain) at 0.44° resolution — covers Paraguay with CMIP5 forcing
- **Eta-CPTEC CONCLIMA** downscaled projections: 1961-2100
- **CMIP6 ScenarioMIP:** Cheng 2019 paper introducing ScenarioMIP recommended by O'Neill 2016
- **CMIP7 in development (2026):** expected release 2028 (WCRP, Chaikalis 2026 plenary)

### 3. SOY/DOLE climate–agriculture coupled

- **GCM-EPIC** framework: simulated soybean yield response in Paraguay (UNFCCC-IPCC TFEIP studies)
- **AgMIP framework** (Rosenzweig 2013) — standard protocol for climate–agriculture impact assessment in South America
- **EPIC-1463** model by Williams (1995) and Song (2022): soybean AGBD-climate response
