## Paraguay context

- 13.6 million cattle (2024), 4th largest herd in South America after Brazil, Argentina, Colombia
- 50% of cattle in Chaco
- Beef exports primarily to Russia, Chile, Israel, Turkey (Egypt/China growing)
- 70%+ of agricultural land use in Chaco = cattle ranching

## Future pathways for Paraguay

- **Havlík 2018 modelling suggests:** Paraguay could reduce GHG by 60-80% from BAU by:
  - Cattle ranching intensification (rotational grazing, supplementation)
  - Forage legume planting (reduces methane per head)
  - Stocking density normalization
  - Manure management (biogas)
  
- **Sin (Princeton's Sourcebook of Interventions) net mitigation potential:** ~1.6 tCO2e/ha/year for Paraguay if all implemented

## Soybean expansion links

- **Pendrill 2019 + 2022:** Paraguay's soybean-driven deforestation dominates
- **Counterfactual:** if no soybean expansion, carbon loss would be ~30% lower
