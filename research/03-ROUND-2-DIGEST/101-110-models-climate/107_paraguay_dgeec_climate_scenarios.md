## Paraguay Demographic and Statistical Office (DGEEC)

DGEEC produces regional projection summaries under different SSPs:
- **SSP1-2.6 (sustainability):** Best-case climate policy
- **SSP2-4.5 (middle road):** Central projection
- **SSP3-7.0 (regional rivalry):** Worst-mid case
- **SSP5-8.5 (high-emissions):** Worst case (business as usual)

### Projections for Paraguay (2080-2099 vs 1995-2014)

### Specific risk factors for Chaco
- **Increased drought frequency:** SSP2-4.5 has +20-40% drought days/yr by 2050
- **Heatwave duration:** SSP3-7.0 doubles heatwaves (8+ days vs 4 days baseline)

## Vulnerability indices for Paraguay

### IPCC AR6 Vulnerability Score for Paraguay (predicted):
- **High for water resources** (drought)
- **Very high for agriculture** (heat stress, drought)
- **High for human health** (Vector-borne disease expansion, heat mortality)
- **Moderate for ecosystems** (Chaco biome shifts)

### Paraguayan agriculture baseline vulnerabilities
- Soybean: high heat stress sensitivity (>30°C yield decline)
- Maize: high drought sensitivity (precipitation <500mm/yr threshold)
- Cassava: lower sensitivity
