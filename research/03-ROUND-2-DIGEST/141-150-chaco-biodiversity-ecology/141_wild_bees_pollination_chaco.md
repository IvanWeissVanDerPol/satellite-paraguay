## Background

Pollinators are critical for:
- Crop yield (e.g., soybean enhancement ~25%)
- Native plant reproduction
- Ecosystem resilience

## Paraguay-specific patterns

### Inventory of Chaco bees
- **Total species:** estimated 200-300 species
- **Eusocial bees:** Trigona, Tetragonisca (stingless bees) 
- **Solitary bees:** Centris, Megachile, Xylocopa

### Drivers of decline
- Land conversion (cattle + soybean)
- Pesticide use (especially neonicotinoids)
- Invasive species (Apis mellifera scutellata competition)

## Implications for Yrupe + Yvutu

- **Yrupe (crop yield):** Pollinators important for soybean self-pollination enhancement and native vegetation
- **Yvutu (deforestation):** Loss of pollinator habitat under agricultural expansion
