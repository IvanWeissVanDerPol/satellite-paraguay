**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Key invasive species in Paraguay

### Plants
- **Brachiaria brizantha** (Palisade grass) — widespread
- **Brachiaria decumbens**
- **Brachiaria humidicola**
- **Cynodon dactylon** (Bermuda grass) — common in pastures
- **Pennisetum purpureum** (Elephant grass)
- **Citrus aurantium** (escaped orange)

### Animals
- **Apis mellifera scutellata** (Africanized honeybee) — affects native bees
- **Sus scrofa** (Wild boar) — small populations in Eastern
- **Axis axis** (Chital deer) — introduced for hunting
- **Cuniculus paca** (native but feral) — controversial in some regions
- **Procambarus clarkii** (Red crayfish) — invasive in some wetlands

### Microorganisms
- **Cypress beetle (Phloeosinus armatus)** — threatens native Cupressus
- **Asian soybean rust** (*Phakopsora pachyrhizi*) — soybean pest
- **Huanglongbing (HLB)** — citrus greening disease
