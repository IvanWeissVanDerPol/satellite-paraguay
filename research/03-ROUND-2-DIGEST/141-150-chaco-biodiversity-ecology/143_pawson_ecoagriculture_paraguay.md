## Paraguay context

Eco-agriculture = integrated landscape management combining:
1. **Agricultural production** (food, fiber)
2. **Biodiversity conservation** (native vegetation)
3. **Ecosystem services** (water, soil)

### Paraguay-specific implementation

#### Payments for Environmental Services (PES)
- **Law 3001/06:** Articles 19-21 establish PES framework
- **Funding:** Limited implementation to date

#### Agro-ecology & Zero Deforestation
- **Zero Deforestation Law 2524/04:** Eastern region only
- **Law 3000/06:** Article 8 establishes environmental quality standards
