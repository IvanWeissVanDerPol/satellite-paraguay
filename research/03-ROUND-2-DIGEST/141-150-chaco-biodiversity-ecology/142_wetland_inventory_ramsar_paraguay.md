## Paraguay wetlands overview

### Major wetland ecosystems
1. **Pantanal extension in Paraguay:** ~2% of country, mainly Central eastern Chaco
2. **Ibera-style wetlands:** Limited presence
3. **Espinal wetlands:** Seasonal, low coverage
4. **Riparian forests (bosques en galería):** Along major rivers

### Ramsar sites (designated wetlands)
- **Laguna Capitan:** 2,500 ha, designated 1995
- **Parque Nacional Defensores del Chaco (Bajo Chaco):** Designated 1995, partly a Ramsar site
- **Ypoa National Park wetland:** Designated 1992
- **Laguna Vera:** Designated 1995
- **Estero del Iberá (Argentina, adjacent):** International recognition

## Wetland carbon

### Biophysical processes
- Methane production in anaerobic soils (significant GHG)
- Significant peat accumulation in some wetlands

### Paraguay estimates
- **Wetland area:** ~10% of country (~4 million ha)
- **Mean SOC:** ~80-110 tC/ha (depth 0-100 cm)
- **Total wetland carbon stock:** ~320-440 million tonnes C

## Implications for Vyrá + Yvy

### Vyrá
- Wetlands are not typically part of REDD+ crediting
- BUT wetland carbon stocks are very high
- Some methodologies (Tidal wetlands) are emerging

### Yvy
- Indigenous territories frequently overlap wetlands
- FPIC must protect wetland-based resources
- Wetland sustainability depends on natural hydrology
