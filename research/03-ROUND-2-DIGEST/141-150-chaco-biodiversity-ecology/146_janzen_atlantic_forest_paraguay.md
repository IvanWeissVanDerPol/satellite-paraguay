## Background

Daniel Janzen (Penn + Biología Tropical + Costa Rica) pioneered tropical conservation models. His framework is highly relevant to Atlantic Forest of eastern Paraguay.

## Eastern Paraguay Atlantic Forest

### Coverage
- **Original (pre-1800):** ~10 million ha
- **Current:** ~1.5-2.5 million ha (80% lost)
- **Deforestation rate:** 0.5-1.5% per year
- **Major remnant locations:**
  - Caaguazú (i.e., Caaguazú National Park)
  - Alto Paraná (Refugio Biológico de Mbaracayú)
  - Itapúa (San Rafael reserves)

### Biodiversity values
- **Tree species:** 1,500+ (mostly Atlantic Forest endemics)
- **Endemic bird:** 19 species in Paraguay
- **Endemic mammal:** 12 species

## Conservation model transfers

### Costa Rica model
- National parks network
- Payments for Environmental Services (PES) since 1997
- **Reduced deforestation by ~75%** in protected areas (later 2000-2017)

### Paraguay's position
- Forest Law 2524/04 (Zero Deforestation Eastern)
- Protected areas (in 22 categories)
- Indigenous community reserves
