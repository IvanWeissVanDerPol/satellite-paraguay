# 148. Paraguay Aquatic Ecosystem Inventory — Riverine & Wetland

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Paraguayan aquatic ecosystems overview

### Major rivers
- **Paraguay River:** Divides the country (Oriental north + Chaco)
  - ~2,600 km long
  - Flows: South
  - Major port: Asunción
- **Paraná River:** Eastern Paraguay border
  - Hydropower: Itaipu (Brazil/Paraguay)
- **Pilcomayo River:** Southwestern border (Argentina + Paraguay + Bolivia)
- **Acaray River:** Eastern (small)

### Wetland categories
1. **Pantanal-affiliated** (Central)
2. **Estero del Iberá** (mostly Argentina)
3. **Estero del Ypoá** (Paraguay)
4. **Laguna Capitan**
5. **Low-lying flooded savannas**

## Aquatic species

### Mammals
- **Giant otter** (*Pteronura brasiliensis*)
- **Neotropical river otter** (*Lontra longicaudis*)
- **Marsh deer** (*Blastocerus dichotomus*)
- **Capibara** (*Hydrochoerus hydrochaeris*)

### Birds
- **Black-fronted piping guan** (*Aburria jacutinga*)
- **Yellow-spotted river turtle** (*Podocnemis unifilis*) (chaco)
- **Cocoi heron**
- **Jabiru stork**
- **Plumbeous ibis**

### Reptiles
- **Yellow-spotted river turtle** (Podocnemis unifilis) — important culturally (also in Kai)

### Fish
