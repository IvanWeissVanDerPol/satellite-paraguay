## Paraguay forestry sector overview

### Production
- **Annual timber harvest:** ~500,000 m³ (legal) + similar for charcoal
- **Major species:** Quebracho, Palo Santo, Cedrela, Aspidosperma
- **Sectors:** Lumber, fence posts, charcoal, crafts

### Economic value
- **Forest sector GDP share:** ~3-5% (large informal sector)
- **Charcoal:** Often illegal supply from native forests

### Sylvopastoral timber systems
- **Combined cattle + timber:** Common in Eastern
- **Forest + cocoa:** Cocoa agroforestry
