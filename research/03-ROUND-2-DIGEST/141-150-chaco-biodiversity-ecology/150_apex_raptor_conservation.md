# 150. Apex Raptors — Harpy Eagle + Chaco Aves

**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Background

The Gran Chaco harbors important populations of:
- **Harpy eagle** (*Harpia harpyja*)
- **Crowned eagle** (*Buteogallus coronatus*)
- **Black-chested buzzard-eagle** (*Geranoaetus melanoleucus*)
- **Black-and-white hawk-eagle** (*Spizaetus melanoleucus*)

## Paraguay harpy eagle state

- **Estimated pairs:** 350-700 (Atlantic Forest, declining)
- **Habitat:** large forest fragments (>5000 ha contiguous)
- **Threats:** deforestation, hunting, persecution
