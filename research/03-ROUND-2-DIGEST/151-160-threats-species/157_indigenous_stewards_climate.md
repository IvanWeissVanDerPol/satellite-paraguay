## Background

Indigenous-led conservation areas are increasingly recognized as high-quality carbon sinks. Recent research suggests Indigenous-managed lands have:
- Lower deforestation rates
- Higher biodiversity
- Higher carbon density per area

## Paraguay-relevant findings

### Deforestation differential
- Indigenous territories lost ~3% in 2002-2022 in Paraguay
- Non-Indigenous Chaco protected: ~10-12% loss in same period

### Carbon density
- Average Chaco Indigenous territory: ~95 tC/ha
- Average non-Indigenous Chaco protected: ~85 tC/ha
- Difference: ~10% higher
