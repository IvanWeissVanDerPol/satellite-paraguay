## Background

## Paraguay montane biodiversity

### Endemic species
- **Atlantic Forest:** mostly lowlands, but with Cuesta de Ñeembucú ridges
- **Yanin-Ypa:** Orqueta elevations (Cerrado borders)

### Elevational distribution
- 100-300m: dominant in Paraguay (~60% of country)
- 300-500m: minority (~25%)
- 500+m: very small fraction
