## Major threats in Paraguayan ecosystems

### 1. Land-use change
- **Driver:** Soybean expansion, cattle ranching
- **Ecosystem:** Atlantic Forest (Eastern), Chaco forests
- **Rate:** 50-100 thousand ha/year

### 2. Climate change
- **Driver:** Increased drought
- **Impact:** Forest mortality risk
- **Region:** Chaco

### 3. Invasive species
- **Driver:** Invasive grasses (Brachiaria)
- **Impact:** Reduces native plant diversity
- **Region:** All

### 4. Hunting/poaching
- **Driver:** Subsistence + commercial
- **Impact:** Local mammal extinctions
- **Region:** Deforested areas

### 5. Water regulation changes
- **Driver:** Dam building, irrigation
- **Impact:** Wetland degradation
- **Region:** Pantanal-affected

### 6. Pollution
- **Driver:** Pesticides, fertilizers
- **Impact:** Aquatic ecosystem damage
- **Region:** Eastern

### 7. Mining/resource extraction
- **Driver:** Gold mining (informal)
- **Impact:** Mercury + habitat loss
- **Region:** Alto Paraguay + Boquerón
