## Paraguay-specific drivers

### 1. Economic
- Soybean price cycles (2003-2014 boom)
- Cattle ranching demand
- Land price escalation

### 2. Policy
- China soy imports → soybean provincial economic policy
- Federal Agrarian Council subsidies
- Less restrictive Eastern Atlantic Forest Zero Deforestation Law

### 3. Climate
- Sahel-style expansion supported by recent wetter-than-normal years
- Climate variability expansion-correlated

### 4. Tech / innovation
- GMO varieties (Roundup Ready + Bt soybean)
- Intensive machinery
- Brazilian capital inflow
