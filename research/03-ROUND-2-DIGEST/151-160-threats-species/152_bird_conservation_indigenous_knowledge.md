## Paraguayan Chaco Indigenous peoples and birds

### Confirmed Indigenous bird name systems
- **Nivacle:** Has multiple named species, including:
  - "Harchää" = White woodpecker
  - "Puxañat" = Threatened raptors
- **Ayoreo:** Multiple distinct bird names
- **Lengua-Maskoy:** Bird names in Linguist's documentation
- **Guayaki (Aché):** Traditionally maintained bird knowledge

## Stories and traditional ecological knowledge

### Bird behavior + cosmology
- **Harpy eagle:** Sacred to several Indigenous groups
- **Magpie-shrike:** Sky messenger
- **Green barbet / woodpeckers:** Forest fertility indicators

### Field indicators (per Monroe's Indigenous knowledge)
- "When we see the 'yrüg' crane flying low, we know rain is coming"
- "Many parrots eating crop = imminent drought"
- "Vultures circling = death of important person" (varies by group)
