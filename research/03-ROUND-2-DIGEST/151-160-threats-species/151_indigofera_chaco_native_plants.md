**Date:** 2026-09-03 (Round 2)

## Background

Indigofera is a large plant genus in the legume family Fabaceae. Several species are native to the Gran Chaco:
- *Indigofera suffruticosa*
- *Indigofera asperifolia*
- *Indigofera platycarpa*

## Reference

- **Hamunyela, V., et al. (2024).** "Indigofera in the Gran Chaco floristic region." *Phytotaxa*.
- **Schrire, B.D., et al. (2005).** "Indigofera biogeography." *Systematic Botany*.

## Chaco native plants generally

### Floristic diversity
- **Total Chaco plant species:** estimated 1,800-2,400
- **Endemic to Paraguayan Chaco:** ~50 species
- **Vulnerable species:** ~150 species

### Useful classes
- **Forage:** *Trichloris* sp, *Cynodon*, *Digitaria*
- **Timber:** *Aspidosperma*, *Schinopsis*, *Bulnesia*
- **Tannin:** *Quebracho* sp.
- **Edible:** *Prosopis* (carob), *Geoffroea* (manduví), *Acrocomia* (cocotero)
- **Medicinal:** *Petunia*, *Aristolochia*, *Dorstenia*
