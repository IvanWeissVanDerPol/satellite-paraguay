## Eastern Paraguay soybean productivity

### Departmental yield patterns
| Department | Soybean yield (t/ha) | Trend |
|---|---|---|
| Alto Paraná | 3.8 | Stable |
| Canindeyú | 3.5 | Slight decline |
| Caaguazú | 3.4 | Stable |
| Itapúa | 3.7 | Increasing |
| Central | 3.2 | Stable |
| San Pedro | 3.0 | Slight decline |

### Yield drivers
- Soil compaction (major issue)
- Climate shift: +0.3°C/decade
- Crop rotation: insufficient
- Erosion: significant in some departments
- Cover crop adoption: increasing (~25% of farmers)
