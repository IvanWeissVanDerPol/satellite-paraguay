### Explore global Indigenous &    Community land rights

Select community-level map layers to visualize indigenous and community lands and territories, or select national-level map layers to visualize country summaries of land rights information.

View introduction

##### Global coverage

As of today, LandMark has mapped **36.8%** of the world's land and natural resources as held or used by Indigenous Peoples and local communities, out of an estimated **50% or more** that experts project is actually held or used. Help us close the map gap by contributing data to LandMark.

* * *

Note that the absence of data does not indicate the absence of Indigenous or Community land.
