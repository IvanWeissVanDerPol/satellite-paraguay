## Paraguay climate context (1995–2014 baseline)

- **Annual mean temperature:** 23.84 °C
- **Annual precipitation:** 1,363.7 mm
- **Temperature trend since 1970:** +0.27 °C / decade
- **Precipitation trend since 1970:** −43.59 mm / decade (drying)
- **Source:** ERA5 reanalysis via World Bank Climate Knowledge Portal.

- **Unconditional reduction:** 10% (with respect to BAU by 2030)
- **Conditional reduction:** 20% (10% unconditional + 10% conditional support)
