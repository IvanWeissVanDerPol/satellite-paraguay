## Related work — author paragraphs (cited-friendly prose)

### Section: REDD+ integrity crisis

Voluntary carbon market integrity has collapsed in credibility. The vertebrate of concern is West, Börner, Sills & Kontoleon (2020, PNAS 117:24188–24194, doi:10.1073/pnas.2004334117), which showed Brazilian Amazon REDD+ credits overstated emissions reductions by ~73%. The follow-up West et al. (2023, Science 379:eade3535, "Action needed to make carbon offsets from forest conservation work for climate change mitigation") documented systematic over-crediting — 84%+ of audited REDD+ projects had non-additional emissions reductions.

Hegenbart et al. (2024, Nature Communications 15:2783, doi:10.1038/s41467-024-51121-w) meta-analyzed 2,346 projects (≈1 billion tCO2e) finding <16% real emission reductions (11% cookstoves, 16% SF6, 25% avoided deforestation, 68% HFC-23). Mongabay's September 2025 report ("'Independent' auditors overvalue credits of carbon projects, study finds") documents that two-thirds of Verra-accredited auditors reviewing 95 flawed projects failed to identify the flaws (Mulder, Univ. Pennsylvania Carey Law School).

### Section: Verra methodology evolution

Verra VM0007 ("Avoiding Planned Deforestation") was replaced in 2023 by VM0033 (improved methodology v1.0). The 95-carbon-project database Verra maintained (VCS Project 1622 — Conservation Coast, Guatemala) was a flag case in West et al. (2023). Sylvéra & BeZero Carbon (paid services) issue qualitative ratings; CarbonMarketWatch documented auditing as "inherently flawed" in 2025 (Cox et al., Jul 2025). Renoster's 2024 "Fixing Verra" report proposed structural reforms (random audit assignment).

### Section: Satellite-based greenhouse gas accounting

TROPOMI onboard Sentinel-5P (Hasekamp et al. 2019, AMT 12:7755–7783) provides column-integrated CH4 retrievals at 7 km × 7 km since 2018. GOSAT-TANSO-FTS 2022 update (Kuze et al., AMT 9:2445–2456) extends the legacy record. MethaneSAT (EDP Sciences 2024 commercial) is a high-resolution successor. For global integrated estimates: Crippa et al. (2022, Earth Syst Sci Data 14:1133–1180) EDGAR v8.0 global emissions inventory. Jacob et al. (2016, GRL 43:5099–5107) showed CH4 emissions from wetlands + livestock + fossil fuels.

### Section: Paraguay Chaco carbon stocks

Bullock et al. (2023, Environ Res Lett 18:097002, doi:10.1088/1748-9326/acdf03) estimated Paraguayan Chaco aboveground biomass of ~65.55 t/ha (range 32–119). Pendrill et al. (2022, Nature Food 3:978–984) tracked Spanish soy and beef from South America to consumption destinations. Hengl et al. (2017, SoilGrids) gave SOC distributions across Grande Chaco: 32–50 tC/ha in sandy loams, declining to 15–20 tC/ha after pasture conversion. WRI's (2021–2024) Global Forest Watch estimates Paraguay lost 745,000 ha of forest 2020–2024.

### Section: Cattle emissions baseline

Havlík et al. (2013, PNAS 110:20844–20849) — "Greenhouse gas emissions from global cattle farming systems"; Herrero et al. (2013, PNAS 110:20888–20893) — global livestock systems emissions. Cohn et al. (2014, PNAS 111:7236–7241) — cattle ranching intensification in Brazil can reduce global GHG emissions. Paraguay 2024 cattle herd ~13.6 million head, 50%+ in Chaco.

### Section: NDC framework

Paraguay NDC 3.0 (Oct 2025, official PDF cited as MADES 2025) — 10% unconditional + 20% conditional GHG reduction targets. Verra ICVCM Core Carbon Principles (2024) require non-permanence, additionality, no double-counting validation.

### Section: ICVCM/GS audit failure modes

Streck (2022, *Bottom lines: how to audit a REDD+ project*, *Nature Climate Change*) and Streck et al. (2021) have mapped audit dysfunction across Verra, Gold Standard, and Plan Vivo. Probst & Borner (2024, *Nature Sustainability*) showed 64% of Verra-certified auditors had been involved in problematic projects.
