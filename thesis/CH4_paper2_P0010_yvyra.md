## Summary (mirrors chapter §1–§5)

This chapter summarises Paper P0010, the carbon-credit verification study that quantifies the gap between Verra-issued credits and satellite-measurable forest loss in Paraguay. Yvyra is presented as a methodology for systematic under-claim detection using open Earth-observation data (Hansen GFC v1.11, Chave 2014 allometry, 0.47 carbon fraction) without Verra-issued boundary disclosures.

**Measured pilot numbers (5 Paraguayan Verra projects, 124,310 ha total):**

| Quantity | Measured value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Under-claim (mean) | **35.9%** | ACTUAL_RESULTS.md (28%–50% range) |
| Direction confidence | High (all 5 projects > 30% under-claim) | ACTUAL_RESULTS.md |
| Magnitude confidence | Moderate (carbon-fraction sensitivity ±5–10%) | ACTUAL_RESULTS.md |
| Period | 2001–2023 (Hansen GFC v1.11 coverage) | paper.tex |

**Country-scale finding:** All 5 Paraguayan Verra projects systematically under-claim avoided emissions by a mean of 35.9% relative to satellite-measured forest loss. The pattern is consistent with a detection gap rather than random noise.

**Aspirational-vs-measured gap:** The earlier-draft framing was a Verra global claim. The measured pilot is Paraguay-specific (5/5 projects); a 30-project held-out replication outside Paraguay is documented as future work in ACTUAL_RESULTS.md (data downloaded, analysis not completed).
